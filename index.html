Vinsea Kid TikTok Poster

An internal content publishing tool for Vinsea Kid — a Vietnamese brand creating educational supplies and toys for children.

This tool connects our content team to our official TikTok brand account (@vinseakidshop) through TikTok's Content Posting API, enabling us to upload and publish our self-produced marketing videos directly — replacing the manual upload process.

How it works

  1. Our team prepares original marketing videos (product demos, unboxing, parenting tips).
  2. Authorized staff log in with the brand's TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit.
  3. Videos are uploaded and published directly to @vinseakidshop via the Content Posting API.

All content is 100% original, created in-house by the Vinsea Kid team.
