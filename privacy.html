Privacy Policy

Vinsea Kid TikTok Poster — Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Overview

Vinsea Kid TikTok Poster is an internal tool used by the Vinsea Kid team to publish videos to the brand's own official TikTok account. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data.

2. Data We Access

Through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, the app accesses only:

  • Basic account info (user.info.basic scope): display name and profile of the authorized brand account, used solely to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish scopes): the ability to upload video files and publish them to the authorized brand account.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or process:

  • Personal data of other TikTok users (followers, viewers, commenters)
  • Location data, device identifiers, or browsing history
  • Any data beyond what is strictly necessary to publish videos to our own account

4. Data Storage

OAuth access tokens are stored securely on our private server and are used only to perform authorized publishing actions. We do not retain video analytics or audience data obtained through the API.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is never used for advertising targeting.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Access tokens are revoked when the app is disconnected. Upon request, we will delete all stored credentials associated with your authorization.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is an internal business tool and is not directed at children. While our brand serves families with children, the app itself is operated solely by adult team members.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: vinseakidshop@gmail.com
