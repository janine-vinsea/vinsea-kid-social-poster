1. Purpose of the App

Vinsea Kid TikTok Poster is an internal content management tool for Vinsea Kid, a Vietnamese educational products brand for children. The app allows authorized team members to upload and publish marketing videos to the brand's own official TikTok account (@vinseakidshop) via TikTok's Content Posting API.

2. Authorized Use

This app is used exclusively by the Vinsea Kid team to manage content on the brand's own TikTok account. The app does not interact with, collect data from, or publish content to any third-party TikTok accounts.

We comply with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service, API Terms, and Community Guidelines. All content published through this app is original marketing content owned by Vinsea Kid and does not infringe on any third-party rights.
